# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primarily casual repeat buyers: people who already know and like RawRed rooibos and come back to reorder their usual size (100g / 250g / 500g) with minimal friction. Secondary: first-time visitors discovering the brand who need to be convinced quickly, without a slow "story-first" scroll getting in the way of a fast purchase decision.

## Product Purpose

RawRed is a small D2C storefront selling organic, unpasteurised, loose-leaf rooibos tea. It exists so a two-person, small-batch tea business can sell directly online with a buying experience that feels custom-built and personal, not templated. Success looks like: a first-time visitor understands what makes this rooibos different (raw, unpasteurised, small-batch, family-made) within seconds, and a returning buyer can get from landing to "order placed" in as few steps as possible.

## Brand Personality

Warm & artisanal. Hand-crafted, small-batch, human — the hand-drawn logo mark (rooibos branch inside a red circle) and the "raw/unpasteurised" positioning should read through in the interface itself, not just the copy. Reference lane: editorial consumer-warm, in the vein of independent coffee roasters or small-batch food brands — warm tones, generous whitespace, photography/illustration-led, personal storytelling (two founding sisters), rather than a clinical or corporate feel.

## Anti-references

None strict. Familiar ecommerce conventions (trust badges, quantity steppers, standard cart/checkout patterns, "you may also like" rails) are acceptable and expected — the goal is not to reinvent ecommerce interaction patterns, but to execute them with noticeably more warmth and craft than an off-the-shelf Shopify template, so the brand still reads through in the details (type, color, spacing, illustration) rather than disappearing into generic scaffolding.

## Design Principles

- **Warmth over sterility.** Every screen should feel hand-made and personal — grounded in the terracotta/cream/ink palette and the rooibos-branch illustration — never templated or clinical.
- **Fast reorder over discovery.** The primary user already knows what they want. Minimize friction from landing to repurchase: clear size/quantity selection, short checkout, no unnecessary steps between "I want my usual" and "paid."
- **Show the craft, not just badges.** Communicate "unpasteurised, small-batch, family-made" through storytelling, texture, and illustration — not generic stock trust badges or certification icon rows.
- **Conventions, executed with care.** Keep standard ecommerce patterns (cart, checkout, steppers) recognizable and predictable, but give each one more craft and warmth than a generic template default.
- **Accessibility as baseline.** WCAG AA throughout — this is a commercial storefront that needs to work for everyone, not an afterthought bolted on later.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA: solid contrast ratios across the terracotta/cream/ink/sand palette, full keyboard navigation through catalogue → cart → checkout, meaningful alt text on product and lifestyle imagery, and `prefers-reduced-motion` support for any interaction/scroll animation.
