Two sisters, one farm, no shortcuts.

Rooibos is usually pasteurised after harvest to extend shelf life and even out the colour. It's a fine process, but it also softens the leaf's natural antioxidants and flattens the flavour into something more uniform. We skip that step. What you get is closer to what came off the bush: a little more variation batch to batch, and a lot more character in the cup.

We're a two-person operation. One of us runs the fields, the other runs everything after the harvest. Every bag is still weighed and packed by hand.

Questions people ask before their first order

What does unpasteurised actually change?

Pasteurising rooibos after harvest is standard practice. It reduces bacteria on the leaf and evens out the colour from batch to batch. We skip that step so the leaf keeps more of its natural character, which is why our batches can vary a little in colour and taste. Always brew it with freshly boiled water.

Is it caffeine-free?

Yes, naturally, not decaffeinated. Rooibos doesn't contain caffeine to begin with, so it's safe to drink any time of day.

How should I store it?

Keep it sealed, away from sunlight and heat. It keeps for 18 to 24 months and tastes best in the first year.
