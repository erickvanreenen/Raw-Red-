# Craft steps
**Use** once, after the hero (concept B). Shows the process as steps, not badges (principle 5).

**Anatomy:** h2 "From the bush to your cup"; an ordered list of three items, each with a photo (`radius-card`, 4:3, lazy-loaded), a step title (weight 600, 1.25rem) and one line in `text-secondary`.

**Layout:** three columns from 768px; under 768px, stacked rows with a small square photo beside the text.

**Accessibility:** `<ol>`, so the order is announced; photos carry alt text describing the scene.
